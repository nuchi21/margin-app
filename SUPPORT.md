# Margin support

## Getting help
Open an issue on this repository: https://github.com/nuchi21/margin-app/issues
Describe what you did, what you expected and what happened, and include your device and iOS or iPadOS version.

## Common questions

**Where are my files?**
Margin opens files where they already are (Files, iCloud Drive and other apps). It does not copy them. If a file was moved or deleted in Files, add it again with the add-file button.

**How do I mark up a PDF?**
Open the PDF, tap the round corner button, then write with Apple Pencil.

**Do I need an account or an AI subscription?**
No. Margin works fully without any account. The AI assistant is optional.

**How do I erase everything?**
Tap the (i) button on the start screen, then Delete all files under Danger zone. It asks you to confirm three times.
