# Margin: Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Margin is a document reader for iPad and iPhone made by Andrew Vlad Volgenin-Kulikoff.

## What Margin collects
Nothing. Margin has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer receives no data from the app.

## Your files and notes
Margin opens your files where they already are (Files, iCloud Drive and other apps) and does not copy or upload them. Its own data (your library list, Pencil marks, notes, bookmarks and settings) stays on your device and is encrypted by Apple's Data Protection. Delete all files in the app erases all of it.

## Optional AI assistant
The AI assistant is off unless you use it.
- Apple Intelligence: runs on your device.
- Ask in another app: Margin hands your question to the app you pick through the share sheet; that app's own privacy policy applies.
- Your own API key (Advanced): if you enter a key, the text you ask about is sent directly from your device to the provider you chose, over HTTPS, under that provider's privacy policy. Your key is stored in the device Keychain, on this device only.

## Children
Margin collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
